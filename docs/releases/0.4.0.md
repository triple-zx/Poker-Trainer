Poker Trainer 0.4 brings the latest training and research tools to the Windows desktop app. This is a **Windows x64 preview**; macOS remains on 0.3.0 until the updated native engine and app are rebuilt and verified there.

- Play full flop, turn and river hands from either position, with animated history, bets and raises, multiple size choices, and feedback after the hand.
- Choose automatic Fast or Deep sizing, inspect retained sizes and alternatives, and practice a specific later-street decision from the saved tree.
- Set opponent tendencies by hand class, edit individual combinations at a decision, and compare the baseline with a response to that fixed opponent.
- Inspect conditional ranges, equity, EV, blockers and every next card; compare saved flops with explicit coverage and weights.
- Edit and import weighted ranges, export complete solved scenarios, and pause/resume supported native solves across app restarts.
- Switch English/Chinese during training and replay without changing the hand. Some research explanations and diagnostics remain English.
- Study up to 96 exact combinations per player when the complete tree fits. Compact storage reduced retained result memory by about 62% on the measured 96-by-96 case; this is not a peak-RAM or universal speed claim.

**Install:** use `Poker-Trainer-0.4.0-x64-Setup.exe`, or run `Poker-Trainer-0.4.0-x64-Portable.exe` without installation. Both contain the C++ engine and run locally without a browser, server, account or GPU. Builds are unsigned. Existing desktop data uses the same local profile; export important scenarios before upgrading. Close the old app before opening the new version. A browser-preview profile is separate.

**Current boundaries:** full-hand mode supports zero effective rake, a pot of at least 4 bb, remaining effective stacks from 1 bb to three times the pot, and up to three raises after a bet. Complete trees are limited to 600,000 nodes and 2.4 million baseline probabilities; many larger-range or deeper trees will be refused before solving. Range editing supports broader inputs than local solving. The measured 319-by-621 experiment is not enabled in production. Representative 8 GB hardware qualification and independent preflop solving remain unfinished.

The fixed-tree gap, finite-candidate sizing comparisons and fixed-opponent feedback have different meanings. Automatic size selection does not certify optimal play over arbitrary legal bet amounts. Nonconverged solves remain labeled. Built-in mathematical range examples are not a verified GTO preflop library, and no third-party chart collection is bundled.

Validation: all 90 local tests and syntax checks pass. The final Setup and Portable payloads were extracted separately; each matches the app source, version and bundled native source, passes the engine hash check, and launches in a hidden temporary profile for a C++ solve, checkpoint-capability check and both worker checks. This verifies the app payload, not every interactive installer choice or representative 8 GB hardware behavior.

The native engine's corresponding source archive, patch, checkpoint implementation, build recipe and license are included. `SHA256SUMS.txt` identifies the two Windows downloads. This preview is built and checked locally; publishing it does not dispatch GitHub Actions.
