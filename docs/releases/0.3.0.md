Poker Trainer now runs as a local desktop app with a bundled C++ solver.

- Train complete flop, turn and river hands, including bets, calls, folds and raises.
- Choose from every precomputed size, including weaker choices, and review each decision's EV loss after the hand.
- Follow animated actions and street transitions; replay completed hands and revisit mistakes.
- Save complete solutions locally and explore the strategy tree without solving during a hand.

Windows: use the Setup executable to install, or the Portable executable to run without installation. macOS: use the DMG or ZIP matching the architecture shown in the filename. These builds are unsigned and are not notarized. Scenarios and history stay on the computer; no account is required.

Full-hand mode currently supports hero out of position, four exact combinations per player, zero rake, stack-to-pot ratio up to 3, and explicit finite street sizes. Both players may raise up to three times after an initial bet. The displayed numerical gap can remain above target after a limited solve. Untested sizes are outside the comparison. Automatic full-tree sizing and broader preflop features are still in development.

Package checks include application tests, a full-tree native differential check with 100 simulated hands, and launching the packaged app for a native solve. The native engine's source archive, patch, build recipe and license are bundled. SHA256SUMS.txt contains package checksums.
